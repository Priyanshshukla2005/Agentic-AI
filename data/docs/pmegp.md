# PMEGP - Prime Minister's Employment Generation Programme (loan + government subsidy)

## What it is
PMEGP is a credit-linked subsidy scheme of the Ministry of MSME. You take a bank loan to start a
NEW micro enterprise in the non-farm sector, and the government pays part of the project cost as
"Margin Money subsidy". This subsidy is back-ended: it is adjusted against your bank loan.
It is run nationally by the Khadi and Village Industries Commission (KVIC), and in states through
KVIC offices, Khadi and Village Industries Boards (KVIBs) and District Industries Centres (DICs).

## Who can apply
- People setting up a NEW unit in the non-farm sector: traditional artisans, and rural and urban
  unemployed youth.
- "Special Category" applicants get a higher subsidy: Scheduled Castes, Scheduled Tribes, OBCs,
  Minorities, Women, Ex-servicemen, Physically Handicapped, Transgenders, people from the
  North-Eastern Region, Hill and Border areas, and Aspirational Districts.
- Existing PMEGP / REGP / MUDRA units with good past performance can get a 2nd loan to expand.

## How much / cost
- Maximum project cost: ₹50 lakh for manufacturing, ₹20 lakh for service/business units.
- Margin Money subsidy (part of project cost paid by government):
  - General Category: 25% in rural areas, 15% in urban areas.
  - Special Category: 35% in rural areas, 25% in urban areas.
- Your own contribution: 10% of the project cost for General Category, 5% for Special Category.
- 2nd loan for expansion: project cost up to ₹1 crore (manufacturing) or ₹25 lakh (services),
  with 15% subsidy for all categories (20% in North-Eastern Region and Hill States).
- New applicants get free 2-day Entrepreneurship Development Programme (EDP) training.

Example: a General Category tailoring unit in a city with a project cost of ₹5 lakh: subsidy 15%
= ₹75,000; own contribution 10% = ₹50,000; the rest comes as a bank loan.

## Documents needed
The official source does not list documents. The online application and the bank will tell you
what to upload (a project report is normally needed).

## How to apply (steps)
1. Decide your project (what you will make or which service you will give) and its total cost.
2. Apply online on the PMEGP portal (application, approval and tracking are all online).
3. The application goes to KVIC / KVIB / DIC and then to a bank for the loan.
4. Complete the free EDP training.
5. After the loan, the subsidy is adjusted against the loan as per scheme rules.
