# Trade License and Shops & Establishments (Shop Act) registration

## What it is
These are local permissions to run a shop or business place. They are given by your State and
city, not by the Central Government, so the rules, fees and forms are different in each State.
- **Shops & Establishments registration ("Shop Act")**: registration under your State's
  Shops and Establishments Act, usually from the State Labour Department.
- **Trade License**: a licence issued by the Municipal Corporation (or local body) for running a
  trade or business at a place.

## Who can apply
Shops, offices, eateries and other business places in a city or town usually need these.
Whether a home business or a moving cart needs them depends on your State and city rules.
Street vendors usually follow the vending rules of the Urban Local Body instead (see PM SVANidhi).

## How much / cost
Fees are set by each State or Municipal Corporation. Check your local body's website.
Under Ease of Doing Business reforms, several States removed the need to renew registration
certificates and licences under the Shops & Establishment Act and Municipal Corporation trade
licences.

## Documents needed
Set by each State / city. Usually proof of the business address, ID proof and photos.

## How to apply (steps)
1. Search for "Shops and Establishments registration" on your State Labour Department website.
2. Search for "trade license" on your Municipal Corporation / Nagar Nigam website.
3. Apply online where available, pay the local fee, and display the certificate at your shop.
