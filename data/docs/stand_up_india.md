# Stand-Up India - bank loans of ₹10 lakh to ₹1 crore for SC/ST and women entrepreneurs

## What it is
Stand-Up India was launched on 5 April 2016. It helps SC, ST and women entrepreneurs start a
greenfield enterprise (a first-time, new business) in manufacturing, services, trading, or
activities allied to agriculture. Every branch of a Scheduled Commercial Bank is meant to give at
least one such loan to an SC/ST borrower and at least one to a woman borrower.

Status note: official releases from 2023 and July 2024 say the scheme was extended up to the year
2025. Ask your bank whether it is open now.

## Who can apply
- SC/ST and/or women entrepreneurs above 18 years of age.
- Only for a greenfield (first-time, new) enterprise.
- For companies or partnerships: at least 51% of the shares and control must be with an SC/ST
  and/or woman entrepreneur.
- You must not be in default to any bank or financial institution.

## How much / cost
- Bank loans between ₹10 lakh and ₹1 crore.
- Up to 15% margin money can come from other eligible Central or State schemes.
- You must bring at least 10% of the project cost as your own contribution.
- The loan is given by banks on normal commercial terms (interest decided by the bank).

## Documents needed
The official sources do not list documents. The bank or the Stand-Up India portal will guide you.

## How to apply (steps)
You can apply in any one of three ways:
1. Directly at a bank branch, or
2. Through the Stand-Up India portal (www.standupmitra.in, built by SIDBI), which also guides you
   from training to filling the loan form, or
3. Through the Lead District Manager (LDM) of your district.
