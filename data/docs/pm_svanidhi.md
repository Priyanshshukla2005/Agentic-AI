# PM SVANidhi - small working-capital loans for street vendors (thela, rehri, footpath stalls)

## What it is
PM SVANidhi is a Central Government scheme that gives street vendors affordable working-capital
loans without collateral, plus interest subsidy and digital-payment cashback. It was launched in
June 2020 and is run by the Ministry of Housing and Urban Affairs (MoHUA) and the Department of
Financial Services (DFS). The lending period has been extended until March 2030.

## Who can apply
- Street vendors / hawkers: anyone selling goods, food items or daily-use items, or giving services,
  on a street, footpath or pavement, from a temporary structure or by moving from place to place.
- Examples: vegetable and fruit sellers, ready-to-eat street food sellers, barbers, cobblers,
  laundry services.
- You need a valid Certificate of Vending (CoV) or Letter of Recommendation (LoR).

## How much / cost
- Loans come in steps (tranches): ₹15,000, then ₹25,000, then ₹50,000.
  Repay on time to become eligible for the next, bigger loan.
- Interest subsidy of 7% per year for timely or early repayment.
- Digital cashback of up to ₹1,600 for using digital (UPI) payments.
- Vendors who are eligible can get a UPI-linked RuPay Credit Card with a limit up to ₹30,000.
- Approval takes about 23 days on average, after verification.

## Documents needed
- Certificate of Vending (CoV) or Letter of Recommendation (LoR) from the Urban Local Body.
- Other details are asked in the online form.

## How to apply (steps)
1. Make sure you have a Certificate of Vending or a Letter of Recommendation.
2. Apply on the PM SVANidhi portal or Mobile App, or get help from your Urban Local Body
   (municipality / nagar nigam).
3. After approval, repay on time to get the interest subsidy and the next loan.
4. Use UPI / digital payments to earn cashback.

The scheme also offers training in financial literacy, digital literacy and food safety
(with FSSAI) for street food vendors.
