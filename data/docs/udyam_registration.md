# Udyam Registration - free official MSME identity for your business

## What it is
Udyam Registration gives a Micro, Small or Medium Enterprise (MSME) an official identity. It is
done online and is free of cost, paperless and based on self-declaration (no documents need to
be uploaded). The Udyam Registration Portal was launched on 1 July 2020. Retail and wholesale
traders are also covered (since July 2021).

For very small businesses without GST registration or income tax cover, the Udyam Assist
Platform lets authorised partners such as banks, NBFCs and other lenders register you as a
micro enterprise and get you an Udyam Assist Certificate.

## Who can apply
- Any micro, small or medium enterprise, including shops, traders, home businesses and service
  providers.
- Micro enterprise (from 1 April 2025): investment up to ₹2.5 crore and turnover up to ₹10 crore.
  (Small: investment up to ₹25 crore, turnover up to ₹100 crore.)

## How much / cost
Free. There is no government fee for Udyam Registration.

## Documents needed
- Aadhaar number of the proprietor, partner or authorised signatory.
- PAN.
- Business details: name, address, bank details and business activities.
No document upload is needed.

## How to apply (steps)
1. Visit the Udyam Registration Portal.
2. Enter Aadhaar details of the proprietor, partner or authorised signatory.
3. Validate PAN details.
4. Fill in enterprise details such as business name, address, bank details and business activities.
5. Submit the application through self-declaration.
6. After verification, an e-Certificate with a unique Udyam Registration Number is generated instantly.

## Why it helps
- Registered enterprises can access government schemes for MSMEs, and priority-sector bank lending.
- Protection against delayed payments from buyers through the MSME Online Dispute Resolution portal.
- Chance to join exhibitions, trade fairs and buyer-seller meets under MSME programmes.
