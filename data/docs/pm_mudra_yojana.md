# PM Mudra Yojana (PMMY) - small business loans without collateral

## What it is
Pradhan Mantri MUDRA Yojana (PMMY) was launched on 8 April 2015. It gives collateral-free loans
(no property or gold needed as security) of up to ₹20 lakh for small businesses.
The loans are for non-corporate, non-farm income-generating activities: manufacturing, trading,
services, and activities allied to agriculture.

## Who can apply
- Small and micro entrepreneurs who want a loan for an income-generating business in
  manufacturing, trading or services, or activities allied to agriculture.
- Shops, small traders, food businesses, tailors, repair shops and other small units fit here.
- Tarun Plus (the biggest category) is only for people who already took a Tarun loan and repaid it.

## How much / cost
Mudra loans come in four categories, based on how big the business is:
- **Shishu**: loans up to ₹50,000. Good for a very small or brand-new business.
- **Kishor**: above ₹50,000 and up to ₹5 lakh.
- **Tarun**: above ₹5 lakh and up to ₹10 lakh.
- **Tarun Plus**: above ₹10 lakh and up to ₹20 lakh (only after repaying a Tarun loan).

The limit was raised from ₹10 lakh to ₹20 lakh in the Union Budget 2024-25; the new limit took
effect on 24 October 2024. The loans are covered by the Credit Guarantee Fund for Micro Units (CGFMU).
The interest rate is decided by the bank or lender.

## Documents needed
The official source does not list documents. Ask the lender. Usually they ask for ID and address
proof and a simple description of your business.

## How to apply (steps)
1. Write down your business idea and how much money you need (use the categories above).
2. Visit a lender: Scheduled Commercial Banks, Regional Rural Banks, Small Finance Banks,
   NBFCs or Micro Finance Institutions all give Mudra loans.
3. Ask for a Mudra loan in the right category (Shishu / Kishor / Tarun).
4. Fill the bank's form and submit the documents the bank asks for.
