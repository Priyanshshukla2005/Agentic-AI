# FSSAI Registration - food licence for small food businesses (pickles, tiffin, snacks, street food)

## What it is
Anyone who makes, sells or handles food needs to be registered or licensed with FSSAI
(Food Safety and Standards Authority of India). Small ("petty") food businesses get a simple
FSSAI Registration Certificate. Bigger food businesses need an FSSAI License instead.
The registration certificate has a QR code, the photo of the food business operator and a
14-digit registration number starting with 2.

## Who can apply
Petty Food Business Operators need FSSAI Registration. These are people who make or sell food
themselves, or are a petty retailer, hawker, itinerant vendor, temporary stall holder, or a small,
cottage or tiny food business, with:
- annual turnover up to ₹12 lakh, and/or
- food production capacity up to 100 kg or litres per day, or
- milk collection/handling up to 500 litres per day, or
- slaughtering capacity of 2 large animals, 10 small animals or 50 poultry birds per day or less.

If turnover is more than ₹12 lakh, you need an FSSAI License, not a registration.
Home-made pickles, papad, tiffin services, cloud kitchens and street food stalls usually start
with FSSAI Registration.

FSSAI registration or license is based on the premises (the place where you run the food business).
Selling food online through your own website or app also needs FSSAI registration or license.

## How much / cost
The fee depends on the type of registration or license and is shown on the Food Safety Compliance
System (FoSCoS) when you apply. If you take help from an FSSAI Food Safety Mitra, their service
charge fixed by FSSAI is ₹100 for filing a registration application.

## Documents needed
The FAQ does not list documents for registration. FoSCoS will show the list while applying
(keep a photo and ID ready).

## How to apply (steps)
1. Go to the Food Safety Compliance System (FoSCoS) at https://foscos.fssai.gov.in or use the
   Food Safety Connect app.
2. Choose Registration (for turnover up to ₹12 lakh) and fill the form.
3. Pay the fee online.
4. Download your Registration Certificate and display it at your shop or stall.
5. Do not run the food business if your registration is suspended or cancelled.
