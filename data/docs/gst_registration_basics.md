# GST Registration basics - when a small business needs GST

## What it is
GST (Goods and Services Tax) is the tax on selling goods and services in India. A business must
register for GST once its yearly turnover crosses the threshold limit. Below the limit, GST
registration is usually not compulsory for a small local seller.

## Who needs it (turnover limits)
These limits took effect on 1 April 2019 (GST Council, 32nd meeting):
- **Sellers of goods**: two threshold limits, ₹40 lakh and ₹20 lakh. Each State can choose which
  one applies. Check which limit your State uses.
- **Service providers**: ₹20 lakh, and ₹10 lakh in Special Category States.
- Small taxpayers selling goods within their own State through e-commerce operators have been
  exempted from mandatory GST registration since October 2023.

## How much / cost
Registering for GST is done online on the GST portal. The tax rate depends on the product.

**Composition Scheme** (simpler, lower-tax option for small businesses):
- Goods: for annual turnover up to ₹1.5 crore in the previous financial year
  (Special Category States decide their own limit).
- Services or mixed suppliers: for annual turnover up to ₹50 lakh, at a 6% tax rate
  (3% CGST + 3% SGST).

## Documents needed
The GST portal shows the list during registration. Keep PAN and business details ready.

## How to apply (steps)
1. Estimate your yearly turnover (total sales in a year).
2. Compare it with the limit for your State and type of business (goods or services).
3. If you are above the limit (or need GST for other reasons), apply on the GST portal.
4. Low-risk applicants can get registration within three working days.
5. After registration, you get a GST Registration Certificate (Form GST REG-06) and must file returns.
